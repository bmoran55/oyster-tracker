How to read a cohort page

Same 12 measurements, every time.

Each update records oyster count, largest and smallest shell diameter and weight, water temperature and salinity, mortality since the last check, fouling on the cage, critters spotted, and any other notes worth keeping. It's not a lab protocol — just a consistent enough log to see the trend.
